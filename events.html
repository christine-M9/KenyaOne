Upcoming Education Outreach

2026 FEB

February – March 2026 Nairobi & Surrounding Schools

School Engagement & Education Support Program

Kenya One will be visiting selected primary and secondary schools to support learners through motivation talks, academic guidance, and identification of students in need of educational assistance.

This program focuses on improving school retention and performance among learners facing financial and social challenges. Our team works directly with teachers and school administrators to identify students who need support.

During each visit, we conduct mentorship sessions, distribute learning materials where possible, and encourage students to stay focused on their education journey.

The initiative also connects selected students to long-term support systems, including school fee assistance, mentorship tracking, and career guidance.

Through this outreach, Kenya One aims to reduce dropout rates and build a stronger, more educated generation equipped with skills, discipline, and confidence.
